## Card Betting Tips Routine

### Player Selection Logic

The yellow card total is a filter, not the selection trigger. Use it to build an eligible pool (top 5 in yellow cards AND top 5 in fouls for their team, plus at least one yellow in last 5 matches). Then pick the player within that pool who has the strongest *current* case based on:

- Recent booking form (when were they last carded, how often in last 5?)
- Active foul rate and combative run of form
- Fixture context (type of game, opposition, home/away, pressure on the side)

**Do not default to the highest yellow card accumulator.** The most-booked player in a team is not automatically the right pick — a player with fewer total cards but a recent booking run and a match that specifically sets them up is often the stronger selection. Season totals get players into the pool; recent form and fixture context decide who makes the tip.
